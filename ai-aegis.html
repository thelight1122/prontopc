AI & the AEGIS Framework

ProntoPC’s AI Technician uses a generative AI model operating through the AEGIS Framework. This page explains what that means for your PC and your choices.

How ProntoPC uses AI

ProntoPC uses a bundled, on-device AI model to help build a repair plan from diagnostic findings and answer questions about those findings. The model runs locally on your PC, without a cloud-AI fallback.

What AEGIS contributes

AEGIS provides the operating framework around the model’s participation in ProntoPC. It connects recommendations to diagnostic evidence, checks proposed actions against an approved repair catalogue, and preserves the boundary between a recommendation and permission to carry out a repair.

The diagnostic scan is read-only. The Technician conversation is screened on input and checked for integrity on output. Repairs follow your selection and consent through ProntoPC’s repair workflow; a conversational response does not itself execute a repair.

What you should know

AI-generated responses can be incorrect, incomplete, or inappropriate. Operating through AEGIS does not make the model infallible. Review recommendations before proceeding, and ask for clarification when a response is unclear.

Report inappropriate AI content

If a ProntoPC AI response concerns you, email support@prontopc.online with the subject “ProntoPC AI content report.”

  • Include the concerning response, or a screenshot with private information removed.
  • Describe why the response concerns you and include the ProntoPC version if available.
  • Share only the context needed to explain the concern. Do not send passwords, credentials, personal documents, or a full diagnostic report unless support specifically requests relevant information and you choose to share it.
