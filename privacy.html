Privacy Protocol

5D Service Solutions Global LLC · Effective July 2026

What We Collect

When you use Pronto PC, the diagnostic scan reads technical system information from your computer — hardware specs, driver versions, installed software names, security settings, event log entries, and storage metrics. This data stays on your PC: the AI that builds your repair plan runs entirely on your device, so your diagnostic information is never transmitted to us or to any server.

ProntoPC is purchased once from the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes the purchase; we do not receive your payment details, and no account or email is required to use the app.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal files, documents, photos, or media
  • Browser history, bookmarks, or saved passwords
  • Email content or message history
  • Keystrokes or screen captures
  • Financial information (the Microsoft Store handles all payment; we never receive it)

How We Use Your Data

Diagnostic data is used solely to generate your repair plan and audit report, and it is processed entirely on your own device by the app's built-in AI. It is never uploaded, and we never receive or store it. Your audit report is saved locally to your Desktop and is yours to keep or delete.

Third-Party Services

  • Microsoft Store — distribution and one-time purchase. Microsoft handles the transaction; we never see or store your payment details.
  • On-device AI — your repair plan and the Technician conversation are generated by an AI model bundled inside the app and run on your PC. No diagnostic data is sent to any AI service or the cloud.

Data Retention

We do not retain your diagnostic data, because it never leaves your PC. Diagnostic results, repair plans, and your audit report live only on your own device, under your control. If you contact support, we keep only what you send us, and only for support and guarantee purposes.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any data we hold about you at any time by emailing support@prontopc.online.
