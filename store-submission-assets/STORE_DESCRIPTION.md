## Description
ProntoPC does what a good technician does — without the trip to the shop. It runs a thorough, read-only checkup of your Windows PC, builds a repair plan with an AI that runs entirely on your device, and walks you through fixing what it finds. You stay in control the whole way, and you keep a full report of everything.

**How it works**
1. Buy once and open the app — no account, no sign-in, no subscription.
2. ProntoPC runs a read-only diagnostic across six areas: storage & disk health, drivers & hardware, security, network, performance, and system integrity. It only reads — it changes nothing on your PC.
3. An on-device AI builds a personalized repair plan from what the scan actually found. The plan is authored locally on your computer, so it works offline and no diagnostic data is sent to our servers.
4. Your AI Technician explains the findings in plain English, and you can ask it questions before you decide anything.
5. You approve the repairs you want — nothing runs without your explicit, per-item consent — and ProntoPC performs them and shows the result of each.
6. A professional report is saved to your Desktop. It's yours to keep.

**What ProntoPC checks**
- Storage & disk health — usage, SMART failure prediction, temp files, caches, recycle bin, large files
- Drivers & hardware — signed, unsigned, and outdated drivers, Device Manager errors, crash history
- Security — Windows Defender, firewall, antivirus signatures, activation status
- Network — adapters, IP and DNS configuration, connectivity
- Performance — CPU, memory, startup programs, power plan, visual effects
- System integrity — critical services, Windows Update, installed programs, event logs

**Private by design**
Your diagnosis and repair plan run on your own PC — there's no account to create and no diagnostic data leaves your machine. The scan is read-only and never changes your system on its own.

**Governed AI — the AEGIS Initiative**
ProntoPC's AI operates under the AEGIS Initiative, our framework for safe, accountable AI. In practice, the AI never acts on its own: the diagnostic only reads your system, every recommended action is checked against an approved list of admissible fixes, nothing runs without your explicit per-item consent, and each step is written to a tamper-evident report you keep. The AI explains and recommends — you decide. It's an auditable safeguard for transparency and non-force, not a claim that software is ever perfect.

**You stay in control**
Every finding and every recommended action is shown in plain English before anything runs. Routine cleanup (temporary files and caches) reclaims space now but naturally builds back up with normal use. Genuine repairs are backed by our 30-day repair guarantee: if a problem we fixed returns within 30 days, we'll fix it again — just contact support with your report. If the scan finds a hardware problem, ProntoPC documents it with the details a local repair shop needs, so you don't pay twice for a diagnosis.

**What you pay**
A single one-time purchase for one complete diagnostic and guided-repair session for this PC (price shown above). No subscription, no add-ons, no trial, and no in-app purchases.

**Requirements**
Windows 10 or 11. No internet connection is required to run the diagnostic or build your repair plan.
