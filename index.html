Questions

Common questions

What does the diagnostic scan actually do?

It reads your system's current state across 35+ categories — drives, drivers, security settings, network configuration, installed programs, event logs, and more. It runs in memory (nothing is installed on your computer) and only reads information. Nothing is changed during the scan.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Your Custodian Technician walks you through every step in plain English. The only technical action is pasting one command — and even that is guided step by step.

What if I don't want to repair right away?

No problem. Your diagnostic report is saved to your Desktop regardless of your decision. Your session stays active for 3 days — you can come back and approve repairs anytime within that window.

What if hardware issues are found?

If the scan detects hardware problems (like a failing drive or faulty device), they're documented in your report with a recommendation to visit a local computer repair shop. The report gives them all the technical details they need — saving you the diagnostic fee at the shop.

What's the 30-day guarantee?

If any issue we repaired comes back within 30 days, we fix it again at no charge. Contact support@prontopc.online with your report and we'll take care of it.
